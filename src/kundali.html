SAV vs. Planetary Dignity Overrides

Ineffective Exaltation

If a planet is exalted or situated inside its own sign/highly auspicious house, but the native's Sarvashtakvarga score for that sign falls below 25, the planet is rendered fundamentally ineffective, lacking local cosmic backing[cite: 13, 14].

Empowered Debilitation

Conversely, even if a planet is severely debilitated or positioned in a malefic house (6th, 8th, or 12th), a high local SAV score (30+ Bindus) empowers the planet, bypassing debility to deliver favorable results[cite: 14].

The Four Pillars of Prosperity

  • 1st & 9th Houses (Lagna & Fortune): Establish physical longevity, vitality, and natural ease of success[cite: 14].
  • 10th & 11th Houses (Karma & Gains): Determine work environment, professional drive, status, and asset conversion[cite: 14].
  • The Dual Extremes: 30+ Bindus in all four houses promises exceptional luxury. Under 25 Bindus triggers lifelong struggle and servitude[cite: 14].

The Equations of Financial Flow

  • Prosperity Standard: Gains (11th) > Effort (10th) & Gains (11th) > Expenses (12th)[cite: 14].
  • Wealth Retention Formula: Retains savings only when Lagna (1st) > Expenses (12th)[cite: 14].
  • The Work Overload Trap: If Effort (10th) > Gains (11th), the native works grueling hours with little reward[cite: 14].
