Average Reference (~28 Bindus)

337 total SAV bindus divided across 12 signs is about 28.1 per sign. Use this as a reference, then compare the relevant house pair.

Middle Band (25–29 Bindus)

Scores in this band are neither marked low (<25) nor strong (30+); interpret them in relation to the paired house.

Lower SAV (<25 Bindus)

A lower score indicates comparatively less SAV support for that sign; it does not by itself establish failure in the house's matters.

Higher SAV (30+ Bindus)

A higher score indicates comparatively stronger SAV support. It is read alongside the paired house, BAV, dignity, and timing.

The Four Pillars of Prosperity

  • 1st & 9th Houses: Compare Lagna and fortune-side support.
  • 10th & 11th Houses: Compare career/effort and gains-side support.
  • Reading: All four at 30+ indicate strong support in this framework; all four below 25 indicate areas needing more attention—not a fixed life outcome.

The Equations of Financial Flow

  • Prosperity comparison: Compare 11th-house gains with both 10th-house effort and 12th-house outflows.
  • Retention comparison: Compare 1st-house support with 12th-house outflows.
  • Interpretation: These relative scores are prompts for reflection, not financial forecasts.
